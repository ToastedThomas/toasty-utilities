How Length Conversion Works

The converter uses meters as its base unit. Each supported unit has a defined length in meters, allowing the tool to convert from one unit to another without needing a separate conversion formula for every possible pair.

For example, one mile equals 1,609.344 meters, while one nautical mile equals 1,852 meters. The converter uses these values to calculate the equivalent measurement in your selected unit.

About the Less-Common Units

Fathom

A fathom is a unit of length traditionally used for measuring water depth. One fathom equals 1.8288 meters.

Nautical Mile

A nautical mile is commonly used in ocean navigation and aviation. One nautical mile equals exactly 1,852 meters.

Chain

A chain is a historical surveying unit. One chain equals 66 feet, or 20.1168 meters.

Rod

A rod is another historical unit associated with surveying and land measurement. A rod is a quarter of a chain. One rod equals 16.5 feet, or 5.0292 meters.

Earth Radius (Mean)

Earth is not a perfect sphere, so its radius varies depending on location and how it is measured. This converter uses an established mean Earth radius of 6,371.008 kilometers.
